Who We Are

Agriculture with purpose.

We are building an integrated agricultural business that connects production, processing, livestock, farmers and markets.

Elekoni is a youth led agribusiness focused on creating value across the agricultural value chain.

Our core focus is the sunflower value chain. We work with farmers by providing inputs on terms, supporting production and creating a reliable market for their sunflower.

The sunflower is then processed into cooking oil, while the processing by product is utilised in animal and chicken feed. This approach allows us to create more value while reducing waste.
